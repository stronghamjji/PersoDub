# Supported languages

**On your computer (10):** English · Korean · Chinese · Japanese · French · German ·
Italian · Portuguese · Russian · Spanish, with the default voice model, Qwen3-TTS

**On your computer with OmniVoice (646):** pick OmniVoice under **Text-to-speech** (an optional download, for personal, non-commercial use only) and the list grows to every language that model speaks. The app listens back to its own takes only in the ten languages above; in the others the voice is kept as made, and a rare language may come out poorly
